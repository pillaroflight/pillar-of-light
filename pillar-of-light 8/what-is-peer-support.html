Intentional Peer Support

What is Peer Support?

IPS is built on a simple idea: you are the expert on your own life. My job is to listen — not fix.

Most support tries to solve. IPS doesn't. It steps back from advice and problem-solving. Instead, it comes from curiosity — listening, learning, understanding.

No hierarchy. No diagnosis. Just two people exploring together in a safe space.

My training gives me frameworks to guide you toward hope and possibility — but it feels natural, not clinical.

Three core principles

Connection

Authentic relationships built on mutual respect — not diagnosis or hierarchy.

Mutuality

No one-way fixing. A give-and-take dynamic where we explore together.

Moving Towards

Focusing on hope and possibility — not just reducing problems.

A fellow patient once told me talking to other patients was more therapeutic than the therapy itself. I felt the same. Less hierarchy meant more room to truly listen.

We give advice too easily. It comes from good intentions — but advice is always filtered through our own experience. What worked for me may not work for you.

We've lost the art of hearing our own inner voice. Too often when we share it, we're judged or dismissed. Validation is one of the most powerful tools for healing.

My sessions are about you. I share my own experience only with permission, and only when it helps you feel less alone.
