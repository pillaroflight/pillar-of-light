What I offer

Services

Listening, guidance, tools, and strategies — tailored to you.

Deep Understanding

I've lived with chronic mental and physical illness for 14 years. I understand in a way most professionals can't.

No Pushing

I have PDA. I know how hard unsolicited advice hits. I won't push. You set the pace.

Flexibility

There's no one-size-fits-all. I help you find what actually works for you — not what worked for me.

Listening

Sometimes you just need to be heard. We can adjust how much talking vs guidance you want — session to session.

Support and Validation

We are often our own harshest critics. Feeling heard and validated can shift everything. Even a small shift matters.

Strategies and Tools

Years of self-study, OT, and building my own systems. I'll help you find what works for your life — gently.

Motivation and Inspiration

Not a kick. Not toxic positivity. Gentle encouragement to help you move from stuck to moving.

Spiritual Openness

I have spiritual beliefs. I don't bring them in unless you want to. If you're spiritual too, you're welcome to.

What I am not

I am not a psychologist or doctor. I am certified in Intentional Peer Support. I can't diagnose or give medical advice.

I am not a life coach. I offer more than questions — I offer presence, validation, tools, and shared experience.
